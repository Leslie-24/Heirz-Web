The Company

Built for Rwanda’s everyday and industrial needs

Heirz Holding Group of Company Ltd is a dynamic and forward-thinking business entity operating in Kigali, Rwanda.

Founded with a commitment to excellence and innovation, we have firmly established ourselves as a trusted provider of diverse services including safety gear of all kinds, hand tools and machinery, lubricants and oil, insurance services, spare parts for all machines, fuel pumps and auto spare parts, solar systems, security cameras (CCTV), stationery equipment, printing graphics and designing, auto-garage services, medical equipment and supply, minerals and fuels, drones, kitchenware, and crushing, screening, industrial grinding and mineral processing.
